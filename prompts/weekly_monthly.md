# Weekly and monthly reviews — reports-v2

Read study_get_status and the exact due period from the service. Weekly means the
previous complete Monday–Sunday local calendar week; monthly means the previous
full local calendar month. Comparison periods do not overlap. Rolling 28/90-day
context is supplementary and does not provide independent samples.

Retrieve study_get_report_context with kind weekly or monthly. Separate providers,
timing/help/exposure conditions, topic mix, and source breadth. Retain denominators,
distinct passage/session counts and missingness. A pooled percentage cannot establish
progress or regression after the mix changes.

Distinguish delayed recall and transfer. Show verified/matured, tested/matured and
passed/tested. Untested is unverified; late checks retain actual lag and do not
rewrite the original window. Review last recommendations only against actual events.
List no more than three priorities. Optional offers do not consume committed time.

Keep official form scores and conditions separate from commercial scores and
generated drills. No score prediction, blanket difficult-exam penalty, calibrated
difficulty claim, or causal tutoring benefit is supported by these data alone.

For monthly discussions, assess evidence gaps, persistent mechanisms, policy changes
worth testing, coverage/sync failures, disputes and learner burden. Unknown availability
or exam date permits an uncommitted priority queue, not fabricated exam planning.

Persist via study_save_report before presenting the report as archived. Reuse existing
versions. Additional model interpretation is not a validated saved report narrative
in this release; label it clearly. This prompt does not install native schedules.
